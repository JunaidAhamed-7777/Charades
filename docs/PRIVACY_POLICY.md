# Charades privacy policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

## Who this policy covers

This policy describes the Charades Android app maintained at https://github.com/JunaidAhamed-7777/Charades. It covers the app itself. GitHub, Android, your device manufacturer, and any external website you choose to open operate under their own policies.

## Data collection and network access

Charades does not collect or send personal information to the developer. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking identifiers, crash-reporting service, in-app payments, or developer-operated server. The app does not declare Android Internet or network-state permissions or request runtime permissions.

Every section, clue, image, source record, and legal document is included in the installed APK. The app does not check a remote catalog, download content, or make network requests to play.

## Local gameplay state

The app keeps the current section, shuffled clue order, reveal timing, guess count, and navigation state locally while you play. Android may retain transient session state to restore the app after lifecycle changes. There is no permanent game-history database, player profile, or saved-preferences system.

Charades does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or personal photo library. Bundled clue images are app content, not images read from your device.

## Retention and deletion

Session state is used to provide and restore current gameplay. The app does not retain a permanent history of guesses or games. You can clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall to remove app-managed local state. No deletion request to the developer is needed because the developer does not receive your gameplay state.

Charades does not provide a cloud backup or synchronization service. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. Backup, restore, and device-transfer features controlled by your operating system or device manufacturer may have their own behavior and settings.

## External links and donations

Clue source references identify the material used. The app displays installed images rather than loading them from source websites. If you choose to open a source/project link, your browser or the destination service may process information under its own policy.

An optional donation action, when configured, opens a separate website in your browser. Charades does not process donations or receive payment details inside the app. No donation destination is shown while none is configured.

Your operating system, app installer, or device manufacturer may separately process installation or device diagnostics. Charades does not receive or control that processing. If you submit a GitHub issue, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be public; please avoid posting private information there.

## Children

Charades does not ask any player, including a child, to provide personal information. Parents and guardians should choose suitable clues and supervise device use as appropriate.

## Changes and contact

The policy included with an app version describes that version. If app behavior changes, the policy and effective date will be updated with the corresponding release.

For privacy questions, use https://github.com/JunaidAhamed-7777/Charades/issues. Include the app version and your question, without sensitive personal information.
